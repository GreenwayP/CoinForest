How investment funding works

Funding sources, account protections and investment rules

Main Wallet

The Main Wallet can directly fund eligible investments. The requested amount cannot exceed the available Main Wallet balance.

Bank Transfer

Bank Transfer can be selected when submitting an investment request. Payment details and reference information are required for review.

Profit Wallet

Profit Wallet funds cannot be used as an investment funding source. They remain separate from the investment payment balance.

KYC Verification

An approved KYC profile is required before an investment request can be submitted.
